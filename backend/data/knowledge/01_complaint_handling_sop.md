# Customer Complaint Handling SOP (SAMPLE DATA)

Synthetic demo document. Not an official Avatar Natural Foods procedure.

## Service levels
Critical complaints (health or safety risk) must be acknowledged by the Quality Manager within 2 hours. High severity within 1 business day. Medium within 2 business days. Low within 5 business days.

## Triage rules
Every complaint is classified by category and severity. Any complaint mentioning illness, injury, allergic reaction, hospital, doctor, foreign material (glass, metal, plastic), mold, or insects is treated as critical or high and escalated to Quality Assurance. Support agents must never close these cases themselves.

## Lot tracing
When a lot code is available, QA checks the production record of that lot and counts other complaints on the same lot. Three or more complaints on one lot within 30 days opens a formal quality investigation and a possible hold on remaining stock.

## Customer contact
Never speculate about root cause with the customer. Never admit legal liability. Offer a replacement or refund according to the Refund and Replacement Policy. For critical cases, send only a holding response until QA approves the final message.
