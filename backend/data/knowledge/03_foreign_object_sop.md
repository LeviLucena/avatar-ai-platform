# Foreign Object Complaint Procedure (SAMPLE DATA)

Synthetic demo document. Not an official Avatar Natural Foods procedure.

## Immediate actions
Ask the customer to keep the product and the foreign object, store them safely, and send photos of the package, the lot code and the object. Provide a prepaid return label for the sample if the customer agrees. Do not ask the customer to discard anything.

## Escalation
All foreign object reports (plastic, metal, glass, bone, wood, insects) go to Quality Assurance. Metal and glass are critical. Hard plastic is high. If two or more reports exist for the same lot, QA places the remaining inventory of that lot on hold and reviews metal detector and X-ray logs for the production run.

## Response guidance
Express concern and thanks for reporting. Confirm that the report has been passed to the Quality team. Do not state what the object is or where it came from until QA completes the analysis.
