# Allergen and Labeling Policy (SAMPLE DATA)

Synthetic demo document. Not an official Avatar Natural Foods policy.

## Undeclared allergen reports
Any report of an allergic reaction, or of an allergen not shown on the label, is critical. Escalate immediately to the Quality Manager and Regulatory Affairs. Advise the customer to stop eating the product and to seek medical care if they have symptoms. Never tell the customer the product is safe.

## Regulatory
Possible undeclared allergens may require a recall review and reporting to the FDA under the Reportable Food Registry. Only Regulatory Affairs decides this. Support must not discuss recalls with customers.

## Allergen facts
The facility handles milk, eggs, wheat, soy, tree nuts and sesame. Each product label lists its declared allergens. Support can quote the label but must not speculate about cross-contact.
