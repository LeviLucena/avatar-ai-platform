# Brand Voice and Customer Response Guide (SAMPLE DATA)

Synthetic demo document. Not an official Avatar Natural Foods guide.

## Tone
Warm, honest and plain. Short sentences. Take the customer's report seriously, thank them, and say clearly what happens next. Avoid corporate phrases and avoid blaming the customer, the retailer or the carrier.

## Structure of a reply
Greeting by first name. One sentence of empathy. What we are doing or offering. What we need from the customer, if anything. Sign off as the Customer Care Team. Keep replies under 180 words.

## Things never to write
Never admit legal liability or use words like negligent, poisoned or fault. Never promise compensation beyond the Refund and Replacement Policy. Never state a root cause before QA confirms it. Never mention other customers' complaints. Never promise a recall.

## Shipping and delivery complaints
Apologize for the delay, give the customer the order number lookup process, and promise an update within one business day. Late delivery is not a quality issue.
