# Cold Chain, Freshness and Spoilage Guidance (SAMPLE DATA)

Synthetic demo document. Not an official Avatar Natural Foods procedure.

## Frozen meals
Frozen meals must arrive frozen solid. Reports of thawed product, ice crystals, leaking trays, bad smell or sour taste are cold-chain complaints. Advise the customer not to eat the product if it arrived thawed or smells off. Ask for the lot code, the store name and the purchase date.

## Escalation
Two or more cold-chain complaints for the same lot or the same retailer within 14 days go to Logistics and Quality for a transport review. A spoilage report with illness symptoms is critical.

## Cookies and pita products
Stale or soft cookies and chips are freshness complaints. Mold is always high or critical severity. Ask for photos and the best-by date and offer a replacement.

## Packaging damage
Crushed bags, broken cookies and torn seals are packaging complaints. They are usually low or medium severity unless contents are contaminated.
