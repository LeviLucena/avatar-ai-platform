# Refund and Replacement Policy (SAMPLE DATA)

Synthetic demo document. Not an official Avatar Natural Foods policy.

## Standard remedies
For quality complaints on a purchased product, the customer may choose a replacement product or a refund of the purchase price. Proof of purchase is helpful but not required for single-unit claims.

## Limits
Support agents may approve remedies up to the value of two units of the affected product. Anything above that, or any request for compensation beyond the product price (medical costs, lost wages), must be referred to Quality Assurance and Legal. Agents must not promise such compensation.

## Retail and food-service customers
Complaints from retail partners (for example grocery chains or resorts) are handled by the account manager under the commercial agreement. Credits are issued through Finance as a credit memo, not as a refund.

## Billing disputes
Duplicate charges are refunded within 5 business days after Finance confirms the duplicate. Customers should be told the refund timeline, not an exact date.
